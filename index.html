Hamara Vishwas

Building Nahi, Pehle Bhavishya Banana Hai!

Hum jaante hain ki abhi humare paas koi bada bhavya bhawan ya sainkdon bachchon ka bada samooh nahi hai — hum shuruaat mein hain. Lekin ek accha kaam shuru karne ke liye bade building ki nahi, sacchi niyat aur sahi disha ki zaroorat hoti hai.

Jab hum sarkar se sahayata mangte hain, to jawab milta hai — "Pehle kuch kaam karke dikhao, phir dekhenge." Hum yeh chunauti sweekar karte hain. Isiliye hum apni har prakriya mein poori pardarshita (transparency) rakhte hain — har rupaya, har record, har niyam janta ke saamne khula hai.
